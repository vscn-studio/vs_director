InGameDevTools (Alodh and contributors)
Source: https://github.com/Alodh1/InGameDevTools
Reference revision: 499cd9d1a37cb687cbb421b1e93715344aa38446

bone handle and IK workflow informed by VanillaAnimationEditor.Viewport.cs
and VanillaAnimationEditor.IkSymmetry.cs. Used in DirectorCreationActors.cs

Reference revision: 13bd62dab1980b974b8ea0f78d037e8e82684b84
License: MIT; full text: BBS-FS-MIT.txt.
Creation editor workflow, shared timeline context, track filtering/folding and
contextual property organization informed by UIReplaysEditor.java,
UIKeyframeEditor.java and UIPoseEditor.java. Independently implemented for
Vintage Story Shape, actor attachments and Director creation data; no Minecraft
assets or BBS-FS runtime are bundled.

Runtime dependencies
Vintage Story and its game assemblies, Harmony, protobuf-net, cairo-sharp and
OpenTK.Mathematics are supplied by the game installation and are not bundled
in this mod ZIP.
VSImGui and its ImGui-related assemblies are supplied by the separately
installed VSImGui mod and are not bundled in this mod ZIP.
OBS and FFmpeg are separately installed tools and are not bundled here.
See OBS-THIRD-PARTY.txt for the companion OBS source plugin's dependency.
