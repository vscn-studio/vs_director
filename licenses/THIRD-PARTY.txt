BBS-FS (McHorse and Wemppy)
Copyright 2025 McHorse
Copyright 2026 Wemppy
Source: https://github.com/Wemppy4/bbs-fs
Reference revision: 13bd62dab1980b974b8ea0f78d037e8e82684b84
License: MIT; full text: BBS-FS-MIT.txt.
Creation editor workflow, shared timeline context, track filtering/folding and
contextual property organization informed by UIReplaysEditor.java,
UIKeyframeEditor.java, UIFilmPanel.java and UIPoseEditor.java. Independently implemented for
Vintage Story Shape, actor attachments and Director creation data; no Minecraft
assets or BBS-FS runtime are bundled.
Timeline track/all selection and keyframe context operations also reference
UIKeyframes.java, independently implemented for Director's creation timeline.
Actor equipment and appearance editing follows BBS-FS's separate actor state
workflow; selection and skin/clothing composition use Vintage Story public APIs.
World-scoped project discovery also references BBSMod.java, FilmManager.java
and ServerNetwork.java. Director independently implements world/dimension
validation and cross-world copies in its own project storage format.
